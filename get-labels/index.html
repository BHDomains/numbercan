The 60-second install

  1. Clean the panel. Wipe with rubbing alcohol and let it dry completely.
  2. Check the temperature. Apply above 50°F - cold vinyl does not bond.
  3. Peel the backing off the label.
  4. Position it on the bin's white address panel, house number level.
  5. Press hard from the center outward for 30 seconds.
  6. Let it cure 24 hours before the bin sees rain or a wash.

Moves happen: warm the label with a hair dryer and it peels off without a fight.
